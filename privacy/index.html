Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play games published by hornsoft ("we", "us"), including Jugaad Tower (the "Game"), and what choices you have.

In short

  • You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or age.
  • Online play and rankings use an anonymous player ID provided by Epic Online Services.
  • The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

hornsoft is an independent game developer. hornsoft is the controller of the information described in this policy, together with the service providers named below for the data they process.

Contact: hornsoft.support@gmail.com

2. Information that is processed

2.1 Anonymous player ID and connection data (Epic Online Services)

When you use an online feature (online matches or rankings), the Game signs in anonymously through Epic Online Services, a service operated by Epic Games. No Epic Games account is needed.

  • Epic Online Services creates a device-based ID for this installation of the Game and assigns a random player ID to it.
  • Your device model is sent when this ID is created.
  • Your IP address and connection data are processed in order to find opponents and connect the two players in a match.

2.2 Gameplay data

  • Your rating and the number of rated matches you have played are stored on your device and sent to Epic Online Services so that rankings can be shown.
  • During an online match, your moves and your rating are sent to your opponent's device.
  • The ranking screen is visible to all players. For each player it shows a nickname and the rating. The nickname is generated automatically from the anonymous player ID, for example "Brave Tiger 4271". You cannot type a name, and the player ID itself is not shown.

2.3 Data stored only on your device

Your single-player best score and whether you have seen the in-game guide are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

2.4 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and asks for your privacy choices, where required, using the Google User Messaging Platform. Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language,
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them, and diagnostic information.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance, and detect fraud. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Game does not ask for your age, so we configure the ad service to apply teen-appropriate ad settings to all players.

2.5 Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The Game does not record audio or use your camera.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply to you.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the Game you asked to play, on our legitimate interest in operating and funding the Game, and on your consent where consent is required, for example for personalized ads in some regions.

4. Who the information is shared with

  • Epic Games (Epic Online Services) for anonymous sign-in, matchmaking, connections between players, and rankings. See the Epic Games Privacy Policy.
  • Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform) for advertising and privacy choices. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other players, who can see the ranking and, during a match, your moves and rating.
  • Authorities, when we are required to do so by law.

We do not sell your personal information. hornsoft does not operate its own game servers. The online features run on the services listed above.

5. International transfers

Epic Games and Google process information on servers in several countries, including the United States. Your information may therefore be processed outside the country where you live.

6. How long information is kept

  • Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.
  • Your anonymous player ID, rating and ranking entry are kept on Epic Online Services while the online features are offered, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to Google's own policies.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalization, in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads. Where the law requires it, the Game asks for your choice about ads and lets you change that choice later.
  • Local data. You can delete data stored on your device by clearing the Game's data in your Android settings, or by uninstalling the Game.
  • Online data. You can ask us to delete your rating and ranking entry. Email us and include your nickname and rating, which are shown at the bottom of the ranking screen, so that we can find the right entry.
  • Other rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to withdraw consent, to object to certain uses, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, or to raise a concern, email hornsoft.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete the related online data.

If the law where you live requires the consent of a parent or guardian for you to use online services, please play the Game only with their consent.

9. Security

The Game uses established services from Epic Games and Google, and information is sent to them over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a feature is added to the Game. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page.
